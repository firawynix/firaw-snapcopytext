# Privacy

Firaw - SnapCopyText processes screenshots and OCR locally on the user's
device. The application does not require an account and does not upload,
collect, sell, or share captured images or recognized text.

The application may contact GitHub over HTTPS to check the public release
manifest. This request is limited to update metadata and is subject to
GitHub's privacy practices. Copying or saving an image or recognized text only
occurs after an explicit user action.

Questions may be sent to `no-reply@firawynix.com.br`.
